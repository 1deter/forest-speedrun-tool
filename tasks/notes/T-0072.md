## The three ideas, measured (forest-researcher, 2026-10-11)

Live, branch builds on the author's install, Slot 5 on the surface, a test
spot with 5 checkpoint boxes, Capture at checkpoints on, uploads off; every
frame of 25 ms or more from `FrameTimer.Spikes` (frame numbers kept), the
parts from the capture's own log line. Steady captures (2-5) per variant.

| Idea | What it does | Long frames per capture (ms) | Worst | Sum | GCs per 5 captures |

Parts (lean): snapshots 122-140 ms (Megan ~20, elevators ~16, sliding
doors ~16, area ~28 (was ~41: two searches, now one), bushes ~14,
greebles ~14, pickups ~15), serialize 39-47 ms (object listing with
`OnSerializing` ~27, 173 components ~8, packing ~4), streaming reload ~35
ms. The serializer itself over several frames was not built: UnitySerializer
keeps its scope in statics, and game frames between `OnSerializing` and a
component's data would change what is saved.

**GC pauses (checker point 3, gotcha 105)** - same spot, 5 captures + 30 s
standing after, `GC.CollectionCount(0)` and the heap around each: control
(captures off) 1 GC (at the restart) and +0.1 MB/s; lean off 15 GCs, all
forced inside the capture frames, heap flat (~295 MB after each), none in
the 30 s after; lean on 2 GCs (both natural, both on capture frames: one
inside checkpoint 4's serialize, 81 ms), ~10-14 MB of garbage a capture,
none in the 30 s after, and the heap ends ~15 MB higher than with lean
off. So lean does move collection work out of the capture: the garbage
it leaves brings the next natural collection (~80-120 ms) closer - about
one per 2-3 captures, landing where the allocating is (the capture's own
frames here), or earlier in play than it would have been. That is one
~100 ms pause per 2-3 captures against 3 forced ~80 ms collections plus a
~600 ms frame in every capture. **Proposed default: lean on.**

**The F-key capture (checker point 4)**: reverted to its old order -
pickups first, then the steps as before (`RunSnapshotStep(PickupsStep)`
first in the one-frame path); only a spread checkpoint capture lists
pickups last. What else differs for it: each step now has its own
try/catch (a throwing keeper is logged and the capture goes on, where an
unwrapped one used to leave `_busy` stuck), and AreaKeeper reuses its
`FindObjectOfType` from `Capture` in `UnenteredSection` in the same frame
(same answer, one search). It still uses the string `SerializeLevel(bool)`
(lean is `light` captures only). e2e launch / restart / restores / timed
PASS on the branch build.

**Recommendation for the author**: ship (a) lean, on by default - a
mechanic-neutral perf switch (the same bytes; `Data/LevelText` tested equal
to the game wrapper's text), worst frame ~600 -> ~150 ms, 15 -> 2 GCs per
5 captures. (c) spread is the only way under ~50 ms a frame; its cost is a
state ~7 frames (~40-120 ms) after the checkpoint (the resumed clock counts
them) - your call whether that is fine for a practice feature; it ships off.
(b) is not reachable without dropping the keepers' notes the restores
depend on.
