# T-0199 Raw FPS: the main camera's draw calls - what it draws, what is cheap to cut

Running notes across sessions (docs/harness.md 11a).

- 2026-10-10 (forest-researcher, branch worktree-agent-afdee61dfe043971a,
  v0.24.269 + a hand-deployed branch build for the probe; release DLL put
  back after): measured live, Slot 1, author's machine (7800X3D / 4080
  SUPER, D3D11, 1366x768, quality 0). Facts: docs/game-notes.md *The main
  camera's draw calls*.

## Method

- **The render thread on the main thread**: the game launched with
  `-force-gfx-direct` (`SystemInfo.graphicsMultiThreaded` False) puts the
  D3D11 submission on the main thread, so the Frame line's MainCamNew
  time includes it: 1.45 ms (multithreaded) -> 2.70 ms (single-threaded)
  on the surface. Every number below is single-threaded.
- **The same pose**: `Time.timeScale 0` freezes wind, animation and
  particles; two shots 1 s apart differ only by the image effects' noise
  (0.38% of pixels by > 24 on the surface, 0.002% in the cave). A
  "localised change" share (diff blurred over 9x9 px, > 12) drops that
  noise: 0.06% surface / 0.000% cave between two baseline shots.
- **A cut**: the group off (camera mask without the layer, or
  `RenderProbe.ToggleRenderers <root>`) for 4 s against 4 s windows before
  and after, a shot each.
- Table: `RenderProbe.MainCamDraws layer|root|shader|mesh N` (frustum + the
  camera's per-layer cull distances; "drawn" = `Renderer.isVisible`, which
  counts any camera and shadow pass - so it over-counts for the main
  camera). Terrain and its grass are not renderers (layer 26 below).

## Verdict per cut

1. **Every layer / root the main camera draws on the surface is visible** -
   no cut there.
2. **GPU instancing** (`ToggleInstancing`): AFS tree bark +0.14 ms MainCamNew
   and a 0.19% picture change; Standard (Specular setup), 588 materials,
   +0.13 frame; Lux Standard Water Flow, 98 materials, +0.35 frame. Slower
   every time (few renderers share a mesh + material outside the trees) - no.
3. **Billboards out of the held light's shadow map** (light mask without
   layer 2 as the proxy): -0.10 ms surface and cave, picture identical.
   Not built: the flashlight (Spot, Hard, range 130) and `TapedLight`'s
   torch light (Spot, range 200) also cast shadows (`LightsWithShadows`), and
   billboards are drawn from 57.5 m (`FadeNearDistance` 57.5 / 100 / 312) -
   inside those lights' range, so their billboard shadows could show. A
   version limited to lights shorter than 57 m (the lighter, 38) would be
   ~0.1 ms while a lighter burns: not worth a switch.
4. **The held lighter's shadows** (-0.63 ms MainCamNew surface, -0.27 cave):
   visible, and already the game's own option (Shadow Level Low and below
   set the held lights' shadows to None - `TheForestQualitySettings.
   ApplyQualitySetting` from `LightFlicker` / `BatteryBasedLight`). A
   runner's choice, not ours.
5. **The surface world while in a cave** (terrain -0.13 + tree billboards
   -0.12 = ~-0.25 ms of 3.4, ~7%, at the Cave 6 spot): identical picture
   there - but cave mouths are open from inside (game-notes *black walls*),
   so near a mouth, a sinkhole or a ceiling opening the surface is in view.
   **Parked - a question for the author** (below).

## Question for the author (parked, unattended run)

In a cave the main camera still draws the terrain (and its grass) and the
25 whole-map tree-billboard meshes. Off while inside a cave: ~0.25 ms a
frame at the Cave 6 spot single-threaded (the render thread's share on a
render-bound machine such as sxczurass's), no difference in the picture
there - but looking out of a cave mouth (or up a sinkhole) the outside
would be missing (no ground, no distant trees). Options: (a) not at all;
(b) a `[Performance]` switch, off by default, labelled "may hide the
outside seen from a cave mouth"; (c) only while far (> ~60 m) from every
cave mouth and opening - needs every opening listed and checked, which
the game does not have (22 `caveEntranceManager` mouths; sinkholes and
ceiling holes are not among them).
