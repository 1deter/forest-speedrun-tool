### Questions for the author

- **Q1 (#2, Medium):** a runner's copy of another runner's spot (a moved
  zone) becomes the spot's current route as soon as it has the newest run:
  the spot page and every plugin's "Website spots" add / update then get
  the copier's zones under the owner's name. Proposed: the current route is
  the owner's newest route (community routes as now); copies stay listed on
  the spot page as other routes but are never current and never served by
  `/api/spots/<id>/foseg`. Alternative: a copy with moved zones becomes its
  own spot (new id). Which, or leave it?
- **Q2 (#9, Low):** strip Unicode format characters (category Cf: zero-width
  spaces / joiners U+200B..U+200F, bidi controls U+202A..U+202E and
  U+2066..U+2069, U+2060, U+FEFF) from runner names, spot names and
  categories when the site stores them? Stops look-alike / reversed names on
  the board, the site and Discord. Proposed yes; side effect: an emoji built
  with a zero-width joiner (U+200D) shows as its parts.
- **Q3 (#7, Low):** an attempt whose report names a **draft** category
  version is judged against that draft and its page shows the draft's rules
  (anyone can write that line in an offline log). Keep (moderators trying a
  category before publishing), or judge / show only published versions?
- **Q4 (#10, Low):** cap attempts per upload and / or runs per runner per
  route per day? One 4 MB upload holds hundreds of tiny attempts; the spot
  page reads every row. Needs the plugin's real maximum queue size to pick a
  number that never refuses a real upload (proposed: 200 per upload).
- **Q5 (#3, Low-Medium, scope):** an attempt page replays its log on every
  cache miss, and the 64 MB cache clears whole when full - ~20 logs of 4 MB
  viewed in turn make every view a replay. Proposed task: write the judged
  view once beside the log (logs never change) and rebuild it only when the
  allow-list or the category version changes. A design change of the
  attempt store - its own task?
