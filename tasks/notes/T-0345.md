## What the archive is

`wrlines.7z` (QA 1559559451588034691, saved to `Downloads\qa-reports\d.eter\`; only the text
was unpacked, to `wrlines-src\`, nothing run) is **Demo Line Practice Tool** by ProtoAus
(working name `wrlines`, proto.bar - so it is also T-0092's "proto.bar/ftesurf"): an injected
C++ DLL for **Momentum Mod** (Source surf) that draws other players' runs as lines in the world.
D3D11 `Present` hook + Dear ImGui; it finds the camera's world-to-screen matrix by a memory
scan, reads the game's own `.mtv` demos into `.wrpath` files, and draws them. Plus
`wrlines-reference/` (two small Source tools it learned from). Docs: `README.md`,
`HOW TO USE.txt`, `docs/how-it-works.md` (4,200 lines of measured design notes).

**It has no replay player of its own.** Watching a run is Momentum's own replay viewer
(`mom_tv_replay_watch`, the tool only copies the demo into the game's folder and puts the
command on the clipboard). The transport HUD the author remembers (timeline, play / pause,
speed on screen) is Momentum's, not this tool's. Its savestates are Momentum's save-locs too.

## Run lines: theirs vs ours

Theirs (`src/wr_render.cpp`, `EmitPath`): every point projected to the screen by the tool,
then drawn as an ImGui **anti-aliased polyline, 2.5 px** thick, alpha 0.85, a saturated
per-run palette; segments near-plane clipped **in world space** (no popping when turning) and
viewport-clipped; a **distance fade** (last 25 % of a 4000-unit draw distance, 12 alpha
buckets) and cull; **screen-space decimation** (a point < 2 px from the last drawn is skipped)
and a per-run point budget (1500) - measured 0.24 ms for 8 lines, 8 ms for 256. A per-line
colour mode: one colour, **by speed**, by energy, by phase - quantised into 16 buckets so a
batch only breaks when the bucket changes. **Breaks at teleports** (found once at load, never
joined into a bar). Markers: a filled dot with a **dark ring** at each checkpoint, labelled
with its split time; a ring with a dark halo on the compared point; name tags kept apart by a
rectangle reservation. **Draws through walls on purpose** (no depth test - "for a route tool
arguably what you want"). An optional presentation-only **lift** (`lineHeightOffset`): points
are the player's feet, some find a line at sightline easier to follow.

Ours (`Game/DebugDraw.cs` `RunLineBehaviour.DrawLines`): `GL.Begin(GL.LINES)` with
`Hidden/Internal-Colored` - on D3D11 a GL line is **always 1 px**, aliased, no width. One flat
colour per line (comparison light blue 0.35/0.75/1, current yellow, failed red, path white),
opacity slider. Drawn in `Game/LatePass` after the image effects, **depth-tested against the
scene** (LatePass binds the scene's depth). Points are the recorded player position (feet),
0.75 m apart (`Data/LineBuffer`), every point drawn every frame, no fade, no cull, no break at a
jump.

**Seen live** (v0.24.305, Slot 1 surface, `spot.my.new-spot-3` "Cave 5 Practice Run", 12
finished runs; run lines switched on over the bridge):
`shots/2026-10-15-t0345-run-line-1px.png` - the comparison line is a 1 px light-blue thread,
barely readable against the bright foggy foliage, cut by plants and the rock (depth test) and
invisible near the player, where it runs at foot height inside the grass (points 0-15 at
y 80.6-82.6 on 80.5 m ground). That is the "crisp and easy to see" gap: width, contrast
(no dark edge), and height / occlusion.

## Replay HUD: ours

`Modules/PracticeRunModule.Camera.cs`: while the replay camera plays, the only on-screen state
is one HUD-column pair, `Replay chase 00:05.324 / 00:06.817 x1`, plus a 7 s notice listing
the keys. The timeline slider, Play / Pause, -1 s / +1 s, slower / faster are in the Runs tab
(F2). Seen live: `shots/2026-10-15-t0345-replay-camera-hud.jpg` - also the big timer
reads 0.00 and the splits panel shows the idle segment, not the replay's time or splits, and
the ghost figure is a 1 px pink wireframe (same GL lines).

## Savestates

Nothing to take. Their "save-loc times" only put a practice clock back when the game's own
save-loc is loaded (Momentum leaves the save-loc's `time` field at -1), and seed the energy
readout from the velocity the game stored. Ours already resume the run clock from a
checkpoint capture's meta, and put a ride's speed back (zipline, sled, glider;
docs/savestates.md). Two habits worth keeping, both already ours: a load that changes nothing
says so; a restart outranks a savestate match.

## Also worth having (lower)

- A **graph** of a run's speed over time / distance: every enabled line plus your own last
  attempt (kept after a fail), a hover reading all of them; buckets keep min and max (one
  sample per bucket hid whole spikes); a median-of-5 removes two-sample spikes from the
  picture only. Ours records `Speed` per sample (`Data/RunRecorder.RunSample`), so the data is
  there.
- **More than one comparison line** (PB, a site run, the WR) each with a name tag, and
  **aim at a line to see whose it is** (the crosshair, not the mouse) - only useful once
  site runs can be drawn as lines.
- Their discipline matches ours (one-place clipping, measured perf numbers in comments, "say
  why it drew nothing" diagnostics, tests that re-insert the bug to prove the test bites).

## How T-0368 could be built (for forest-dev; perf must be measured live)

GL lines cannot be wider than 1 px on D3D11, and `LineRenderer` draws before the image
effects (LatePass exists to draw after them). So, inside `RunLineBehaviour.DrawLines`
(still LatePass, depth-tested): per segment, the side vector
`normalize(cross(b - a, cameraPos - mid))` scaled by `width_px * distance * 2 tan(fov/2) / screenHeight`
gives a quad of constant pixel width (`GL.QUADS` or `GL.TRIANGLES`); a dark outline is the same
quad 2 px wider drawn first in black at ~0.6 alpha; the AA edge an extra strip each side going
to alpha 0. Keep it cheap the way theirs does: cull by distance first, skip points < 2 px
apart on screen, clip in world space at the near plane before projecting, and only the
window (`LineBuffer.Window`) when Ahead-only is on. Measure with `DrawTarget.Record` /
`Frame (30 s):` on the 154-run Labskip spot (gotcha 42: baseline on a fresh launch).
