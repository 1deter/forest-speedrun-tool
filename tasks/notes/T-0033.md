## Question for the author (parked, unattended run)

The game-side sources a behaviour-preserving patch could remove are all
small: ocean condition key 3.8 KB/s (`Ceto.WaveSpectrum.NewSpectrumConditionKey`,
reuse the last key when its inputs match - keys are immutable and compared
by value), cave spawn sort 7.5 KB/s in caves only (`mutantController.
sortCaveSpawnsByDistance`, a cached comparer), fish spawning 3.2 KB/s

invalidation - not provably the same). Together ~10-15% of the in-play
figure, which already gives a GC only every ~30 min. Ship the first two as
`[Performance]` switches (on by default, one log line each), or leave the game
alone here and spend the effort on the restart loop (~40 MB and 1-2 forced
GCs per Quick load, the pauses runners actually see)? Recommendation: leave
them; open a task for the restart loop.
