# T-0149 - the endgame's Material leak

Cause, proof and numbers: docs/game-notes.md *The endgame leaks one
Material a frame*. Fix: `PerfPatches` switch `SunPostProcessKeepMaterial`
(Debug views -> Performance, on by default, behaviour-preserving).

Before (bridge, v0.24.252, 2026-10-08, author's PC, ~280-300 fps):
- Material count +1 per frame in the endgame lab (+3093 / 3093 frames;
  +1511 / 1511 after a plain Slot 1 load, no restore); 0 on the surface
  and in Cave 6.
- The leaked materials are freed by the next UnloadUnusedAssets; that
  sweep on leaving the lab took 446 ms with ~103k leaked vs 337 ms with
  ~1.5k (~1.1 ms per 1000) - at the 01-57 report's 1.47M, ~1.5 s more
  on the sweep, plus the 1.47M objects every FindObjectsOfTypeAll walks.

To prove on the released build (forest-tester):
1. Log: `Performance patch 'SunPostProcessKeepMaterial': on.`
2. Load Slot 1 (it is in the lab) or restart the Labskip spot; then
   `call static:ForestOverlay.Game.RenderProbe TextureUsers a _MainTex`,
   wait 5 s, again: the two "in N materials" counts are equal (were +1 a
   frame), and `call camera SunshinePostprocess.blitMaterial.GetInstanceID`
   twice 0.5 s apart returns the same id. The log has `Performance: the
   sun post-process was switched on again - kept its material ...` once.
3. Switch it off (Debug views, or `TogglePerfPatch` by index): the count
   grows again by the frame count; on again: flat.
4. A screenshot in the lab with it on and off: the same picture.

Question for the author (not blocking): the switch's label and text are
runner-facing words - "Endgame: stop the sun post-process making a
material every frame" (same style as the other Performance switches).
Keep, or reword?
