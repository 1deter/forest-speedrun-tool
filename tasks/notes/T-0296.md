# T-0296 - paint hidden by the surface it lies on

Author, 2026-10-10 (T-0219 branch build): parts of strokes on a cave wall
and on the ground vanish; "this either changes based on look angle or
position"; a green rock simply doesn't show the paint. "Not a big deal ...
worth noting to see if anything can be done about it in the future."
Shots: the session's images 1.webp (cave wall, "SKIP" in green) and 2.png
(red line on ground with gaps).

Theories (unproved - check live before building on them):
- The ray hits the **collider**, not the drawn mesh: a rock's / cliff's
  collider is a simplified shape inside or behind its render mesh, so the
  paint lies behind the visible surface and the depth test hides it.
- **LOD**: rocks / terrain swap meshes with distance and angle; the drawn
  surface moves past the fixed paint (fits "changes with look angle or
  position"). Terrain pixel error also changes with distance.
- Ribbons are flat between two dots: on bumpy ground the middle of a long
  segment can dip under a bump.
- The lift is fixed (1.5 cm + `_ZBias` -2); depth precision far away.

Ideas: a lift that grows with distance from the camera, drawn per frame in
the shader (or ZTest with a larger view-space offset); denser dots on
terrain (sample the terrain height between dots); an option to draw paint
on top (no depth test) within a few metres of the surface.
