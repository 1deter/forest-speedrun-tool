## Tried and taken out

A `[Performance] RestoreCollectWhenDue` switch (commit 572db94, reverted
in 1706109): a transpiler sent `LevelLoader.Load`'s `GC.Collect()` through
a 64 MB budget during our in-place loads. 10 restores: GCs 10 -> 4,
"done in" 130 -> 49 ms when skipped - but 2 of the 4 came from volume
(Boehm's trigger here is ~63-78 MB), one 4 s after a restore, i.e. in
play. A smaller budget collects every restore at ~28 MB a restore. So it
is a trade-off, parked as **T-0280** (question for the author).
