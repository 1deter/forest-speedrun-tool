# T-0351 Paint: a stroke behind an object erases paint on the object's near side

Running notes across sessions (docs/harness.md 11a).

- 2026-10-15, author (branch build 749a7c46, t-0296-paint-surface): "the paint
  works good", but with paint on both sides of a tree trunk the near stroke
  disappears along the line the far side's stroke takes ("cancelling out").
  Two shots in that chat: red strokes on a trunk with a gap that follows the
  hidden stroke behind it.
- Cause (read from the code, not yet proved live): `PaintDraw.DrawLate`
  (src/Game/PaintDraw.cs ~L313-342, materials ~L271-273) draws paint into a
  temporary RT against the scene depth in two passes over ALL meshes:
  `_bandKeep` (One/Zero, ZTest GreaterEqual) with the meshes scaled about the
  eye by 1 + Band, then `_bandCut` (Zero/Zero, ZTest Greater) scaled by
  1 - Band, which clears every pixel where the scene is in front. The far
  side's stroke, pulled 2 % toward the eye, is still behind the trunk's
  front, so its cut pass passes the depth test and wipes the near stroke's
  pixels already in the RT. The cut is global across all paint, not per dot.
- Fix ideas (unproved): one pass with a per-fragment band test (sample
  `_CameraDepthTexture` in the shader, keep only where |scene - paint| <=
  band); or a stencil per stroke; or keep + cut per dot, back to front.
- Related: T-0296 (blocked by the checker 2026-10-15) and T-0299 - same
  design, same commit.
- 2026-10-15 (T-0296 revise session): the late pass now draws first and clears after, the clear only where paint lies in front of the scene - a stroke behind the trunk clears nothing. Built on t-0296-paint-surface, not yet seen live; see T-0296 notes.
