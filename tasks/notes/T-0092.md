**proto.bar/ftesurf** - ProtoAus's Demo Line Practice Tool (Momentum Mod);

**jrik.dev** - a Half-Life speedrun hub, three groups:
- *Tools*: Half-Life Demo Analyzer (in-browser 3D replay of the runner's own
  demos: speed / gain / prestrafe / keys overlays, 0.1x-8x stepping, a
  compare mode with a see-through ghost or side by side synced map by map
  with splits and delta, a "SoB my runs" mode that stitches the best map of
  up to 10 runs and replays it, a free-fly map explorer); AG Demo Analyzer
  (HLKZ strafe loss).
- *Games*: HLGUESSR (a screenshot from a real run: guess the chapter, pin the
  spot on the chapter's top-down map - up to 5,000 points by distance - or
  watch a 1-min snippet and guess the run's final time; difficulty = how long
  the shot shows; a Twitch-chat mode where chat guesses and a session
  leaderboard scores them); WHODUNRUN (guess the runner from a replay
  snippet); LAMBDLE (daily word game, 6 tries).
- *Training*: single-trick drills, a jumpbug fps_max finder.
- No Discord, no 1v1 / challenge links, no room codes, no LLM on any page
  read; the "click a link to join" the author remembered is not on these pages.
