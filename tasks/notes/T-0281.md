  of the `.run` (plugin v0.24.243+), empty for older runs (`Runs.Run`;
  tested in `ApiTests.Upload_ThenBoardShowsEachRunnersBest`).
- The 3D view's **First person** button beside Follow (one or the other;
  `mapViews` in `app.js`, `Map3D` mode `"eyes"` in `map3d.js`): the camera at
  the focused run's path point + the recorded eye, looking along the
  recorded look - interpolated between samples (yaw the short way round),
  not smoothed, on the same clock as the scrub bar / play / speed (it reads
  `Map3D.time`, as Follow does). No ball over the view's own run; the
  terrain fades when that run is underground (as Follow). Drag turns the
  view around the runner's look, double-click goes back to it; no zoom /
  pan. Back to orbit: 8 m behind the eye.

  world* gives for the author's game at 16:10 (a like-for-like setting);
  orbit / Follow keep 55. See Q2.
- **The attempt page** (T-0223's route replay) now has the same 2D / 3D
  switch, Follow and First person, on its scrub bar, and a click on the
  line sets the time (as on the spot page). **But the attempt log has no
  look track** (only `step` lines, 1 Hz, position): its first person faces
  the way the route moved (over one sample's spacing either side), level,
  eye 1.6 m - the in-game replay camera's rule for a run without a look
  (`ReplayCamera.PoseAt` / `DefaultEye`) - and a note under the view says
  "No look recorded: facing the way it moved" (proposed). The same
  fallback covers spot runs from before v0.24.243. See Q1.
