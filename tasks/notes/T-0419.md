(`Fire2._lit`, `enableEffigy.lightBool`) but not what lighting turned on, and
most burning things are not in the save at all. Each case was captured, lit
over the bridge, and restored (`t419a`..`t419j`). Full table and the game's
code: docs/game-notes.md *Fire, and what a Quick load leaves of it*.

## Fix (build plan for forest-dev)

A new `src/Game/FireKeeper.cs` (game names stay in src/Game), installed with
the other keepers. Rule (decisions.md *Mirror essentially everything*): a
Quick load puts out every fire lit after the capture and leaves what burned
at the capture. A file from another world puts out every fire, as a load
would. Nothing here runs on a Full load (the scene is fresh).

### 1. Hooks (installed at plugin start, never skip the original, never throw)
- Prefix `FireDamage.Burn()` (protected virtual; patch the `FireDamage`
  method, `FireDamageTree` overrides it) and prefix `FireDamage.Ignite(Vector3,
  int)`. Skip when `isBurning` is already True (Ignite returns early then).
  Otherwise record the instance in a registry `Lit` (instance -> seq), with
  `seq = NatureKeeper`'s `++_seq` (expose `NatureKeeper.NextSeq()`), so the
  existing `bushes` mark (`world:seq`) also marks the fires. No new header.
- In the same prefix, while `PickupKeeper.Armed`: if the view's `LodBase` is
  non-null, keep a copy of its scene object exactly as `CutPrefix` does.
  Factor CutPrefix's body into `NatureKeeper.KeepLod(Component lod, int seq,
  GameObject myCut)`. Read `LodBase` through `typeof(FireDamage)` (a private
  field: `GetField` on `FireDamageTree` misses it). `KeepLod` already skips
  trees (`DestroyInsteadOfDisable` false), greeble bushes (root `Pooling`) and
  ids already kept. Record `MyBurnt.name + "(Clone)"` as CutNames does for
  MyCut.
  - Order matters: `Burn` nulls `LodBase` before `Ignite`, so the Burn
    prefix keeps the copy. The spread's Ignite keeps it too, because its
    bush's view is destroyed when the burn finishes (IL: `FinishedBurning`
    destroys the view, then the LOD destroys itself as after a cut; not
    seen live, so check it in the build's own test).
  - A copy whose original is still alive at the restore is dropped by
    `PutBushesBack`, as now. So a bush put out mid-burn by step 3 (spread
    path, LOD alive) is not doubled.
- Postfix `Fire2.On()` (private): registry `LitFires`. Postfix
  `enableEffigy.lightEffigyReal()`: registry `LitEffigies`. These two need no
  seq: their capture state comes from the save or the `fires` line.
- Prune dead entries at each capture / restore.

### 2. Capture (case 7, beside `CaptureMark`)
New header line `fires`: the key of every `Fire2` in `LitFires` that is
`Lit` now. Key: `path@x,y,z` as NatureKeeper's `KeyOf` (cave fires repeat
their names). Pure parse / format in `src/Data` (e.g. `Data/LitFires.cs`)
with tests; `SavestateFile` line `fires` (written when non-empty, parsed,
copied in `Copy()`).

### 3. Before LoadNow (`SavestateBridge.RestoreInPlace`, next to MirrorRoots)
- Every `LitEffigies` entry: `CancelInvoke("die")` and
  `CancelInvoke("ShutdownFire")`. LoadNow's `OnDeserialized` then invokes
  them again for the ones lit at the capture, as a load does, and only once.

### 4. After LoadNow (SavestateModule, beside `_nature.Restore`; before it, so
the restored bush copies never meet a live fire)
- **FireDamage**: for each `Lit` entry still alive with `isBurning` and `seq >
  since` (since = the `bushes` mark's number for this world, else -1 = all):
  `enabled = false; enabled = true;` then `usedFuelSeconds = 0` and
  `SmokeShown = false`. If its `LodBase` is null and a pool spawned it
  (`transform.parent`'s `SpawnPool.IsSpawned`), `Despawn` it: it is an
  orphan, and NatureKeeper puts its scene object back. Then remove roots
  named in the MyBurnt set with an id after the T-0411 mark (the same rule as
  T-0416's thrown things), or leave them: they go by themselves in ~3 s.
- **Fire2**: for each `LitFires` entry: when it is not in the file's `fires`
  line and it shows fire (`Lit`, or `FlamesAll.activeSelf`), call `Off()`,
  then set `ModelLit` inactive and `ModelUnlit` (if any) active: the
  never-lit look. Without a `fires` line (an old file), handle only those
  whose `Lit` is False but whose `FlamesAll` is active (the built-fire case).
  For entries in the `fires` line that are not `Lit` now (a cave fire that
  burnt out since): `Action_LightFire()` (public).
- **Effigies**: each `LitEffigies` entry with `lightBool` False and a
  `fires[]` object active: set `fires[]` and `effigyRange` inactive and
  `BirdMarkers` active. The reverse of `lightEffigyReal`.
- **Player**: in `Wash()`, after `GotCleanReal`,
  `PlayerStats.CancelInvoke("StopBurning")`.
- **Animals**: in MirrorRoots's walk, a kept `CoopAnimal` root's
  `animalHealth` (find it on the root or its children) with `Fire` active or
  `IsInvoking("HitFire")`: `cancelFire()` + `CancelInvoke("cancelFire")`.
- **Cannibals**: after `EnemyKeeper.RestoreCave` has placed the kept cave
  members, for each member's `EnemyHealth` with `onFire`: `disableBurn()` +
  `CancelInvoke("disableBurn")`. Surface cannibals need nothing (the re-setup
  despawns them).

Animals and cannibals on fire at the capture are put out too (their state is
not recorded); see the decisions.

### 5. Done line
`| fire: N put out (tree x1, bush x2, fire x1, effigy x1, cannibal x1,
animal x1)` and `, M orphan bush view(s) removed`, in the module's restore
line; omit it when nothing changed. One `FireKeeper: hooked (k/5)` line at
install. `python scripts/log-catalogue.py` if a new prefix appears.

### 6. Docs
docs/savestates.md: a paragraph after the T-0418 ones (what is put out, the
`fires` line, what stays). Name the gaps left.

### Tests
- `LitFiresTests` (the `fires` line): round trip, invariant culture, an
  empty line, garbage gives an empty set.
- `SavestateFileTests`: `fires` round-trips; absent gives null / empty.
- If "put out or not" (seq vs mark, another world) is a pure function, put
  it in `src/Data` with a test (`FireMark.PutOut(seq, since)`).

## Decisions for the author

- **Settled by the mirror rule** (decisions.md, 2026-10-18): fires lit since
  the capture go out; fires burning at the capture keep burning (each with
  its current fuel, not the captured one). A bush lit before the capture and
  burnt down since stays gone, like a bush cut before the capture.
- **Open (defaults in the plan, change if wrong):** cannibals and animals on
  fire *at the capture* are put out too. Recording them would need their
  fire timers in the file; a capture during a burn is rare.
- **Gaps left (new tasks?):** a kept animal's health is not put back (the
  rabbit stayed at 1 of 4; T-0415 keeps animals as they are). A cannibal's
  burnt skin stays burnt; the game itself never puts it back
  (`defaultMat` null).
