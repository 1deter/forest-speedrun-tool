Running notes across sessions (docs/harness.md 11a).

## Causes (bridge, v0.24.346, Slot 1, 2026-10-18)

**1. A half-chopped tree at capture is regrown by a Quick load - by our
own `NatureKeeper.RegrowTrees`.** `PineTreeThin_414` (CoopTreeId 2543,
view at 423.59, 76.88, -7.86 near the tree spot 428, 78, -4): `call
<view> TreeHealth.Hit` swapped in `PineTreeThinCut(Clone)` (LOD_Trees
disabled, `CurrentView` = the cut); `TreeCutChunk.Hit 1` x2 on `TreeDmg1`
(damage 2) and `Hit 4` on `TreeDmg2` (its parent `2` destroyed, Health
4 -> 3). Captured, Quick load at once: `trees: 1 regrown (not cut at
capture; 1 half-chopped or falling)`, LOD enabled, view
`PineTreeThin_High(Clone)` - the tree, untouched since the capture,
stood up. RegrowTrees regrows every disabled tree not on
`CutDownTreeIds`, and the save's list only holds trees disabled with
`CurrentView == null`, so a half-chopped one always qualifies. A Full
load of the same state: a standing view 2 s after (the game's own;
nothing saves the chop). Gotcha 35 (decide against the capture): the
restore should leave it half-chopped, Health and chunks as captured.

**2. A new sapling stick survives a Quick load when a captured stick
lies within 2.5 m of it.** Capture, then cut `Sapling1_298` (383.61,
75.18, 294.02): sticks at (382.5, 75.35, 293.98) and (381.95, 75.34,
295.31); greeble stick `Pool_Greebles/Stick_High(Clone)006` at (384.37,

`RemoveExtra(present, IsStickItem, UnderACut, false)` builds `captured`
from EVERY stick in the file but `live` only from sticks under a cut, so
a greeble stick's captured entry is free to "account for" a new sapling
stick within `MatchNear` (2.5 m).

**3. A Full load re-cuts a sapling cut at capture without its sticks.**
Cut `Sapling1_307` (385.17, 74.79, 277.14): `Sapling1_Cut(Clone)` at the

## Mechanisms proved live (the fix rests on these)

- **Sapling sticks back**: `Instantiate(<High prefab>.CutBush2.MyCut,
  burstPos, burstRot)` at the captured burst pose gave a
  `Sapling1_Cut(Clone)` whose two sticks landed on the very positions
  they had at capture (386.33, 74.86, 277.43 / 386.79, 74.85, 277.39)
  within 4 s. The MyCut prefab is reachable without a spawned view:
  `LOD_Base.High` (`Sapling1_High`, a prefab Transform) ->
  `CutBush2.MyCut` (`Sapling1_Cut`), `.MyBurstPos.localPosition` (-1.16,
  3.39, -0.4), `.localRotation`, `.sapling` true. A spawned view sits at
  the LOD's position and rotation with scale 1 (LOD scale 0.8, view 1):
  burst pose = `lod.position + lod.rotation * localPos`, rotation
  `lod.rotation * localRot` - computed (383.24, 78.57, 295.19) vs the live
  view's (383.23, 78.57, 295.19), yaw 88.81 both. The sticks carry
  `destroyAfter` (60 s, then every 10 s: gone when the player is > 20 m
  away) - a capture after that lists none, and the removal pass takes
  the respawned ones.
- **Half-chop back**: on the standing view, `call <view>
  TreeHealth.DoSpawnCutTree` (public) swapped in a fresh
  `PineTreeThinCut(Clone)` (LOD_Trees disabled, `CurrentView` = it) - the
  game's own first-hit path. Then, with no hit: `Destroy` chunk parent
  `2` (as `TreeCutChunk.Hit` does at damage 4), `TreeHealth.Health = 3`,
  `TreeDmg1.damage = 2`, `Fake1` (`Chunk1`) off, `Fake3` on. That tree
  chopped on normally: `TreeCutChunk.Hit 4` on chunks 3 and 4 (Health 1),
  `Hit 2` on chunk 1 -> fell (`CurrentView` null, LOD disabled) and
  `OnSerializing` put 2543 first on `CutDownTreeIds`.
  Chunk layout: the cut model's children `1`..`4`, each holding
  `TreeDmg<n>` (`TreeCutChunk`: public `damage`, `Fake1..Fake4`,
  `mapleTree`) and `Fake2..4`; `Fake1` is the chunk's own `Chunk1`
  child. `TreeCutChunk.Hit(amount)`: damage += amount (x2 on a maple, so
  the stored `damage` already includes it); [1,2) Fake2 on, Fake1 off;
  [2,3) Fake3 on, Fake1-2 off; [3,4) Fake4 on, Fake1-3 off; >= 4
  `MyTree.Hit` and the chunk's parent destroyed.

## Build plan (forest-dev)

**A. Sticks matched against every live stick (cause 2) -
`Game/PickupKeeper.RemoveExtra`.** Separate the match pool from the
removal filter: `live` = every active, identifier-less pickup `item`
accepts (greeble sticks included), matched by `PickupMatch.Match(captured,
live, MatchNear, farToo)` as now; only the unmatched ones `where`
accepts (`UnderACut`) are removed. Logs and the wreck keep today's
behaviour (their `where` is already "all" / "under the hull"). Test: a
pure helper in `Data/PickupMatch` (e.g. `Removable(captured, live,
removable[], near)`) with the case above - the captured greeble pairs
with its live greeble at 0 m, both new sapling sticks unmatched.

**B. A Full load puts back a cut sapling's sticks (cause 3) -
`Game/NatureKeeper.ApplyCuts`.** Before destroying a re-cut LOD object
whose `High` prefab has `CutBush2` with `sapling` true: take the burst
pose (the live view's `MyBurstPos` when `CurrentLodTransform` is up,
else the prefab's local pose through the LOD's position / rotation, as
above), `Instantiate(MyCut, pos, rot)`, add `MyCut.name + "(Clone)"` to
`CutNames` (so `UnderACut` sees its sticks with no armed cut this
session). Then ~3 s later (the sticks fall 3.3 m; the captured positions
are where they settle) run the stick pass of A with the file's
`Pickups`: sticks taken before the capture go, the rest stay. Both
callers: the Full load (`SavestateModule` ~line 1785) and a Quick load
from another world (`NatureKeeper.Restore` -> `ApplyCuts`); in the Quick
path the existing stick pass (`SavestateModule` ~line 1497) runs before
fresh sticks settle - run it once more after 3 s. Log: extend `bushes: n
cut again (cut at capture)` with `, m sapling(s) dropped their sticks
again`; the later pass logs with the existing `removed n sapling
stick(s)` wording.

**C. Half-chopped trees as captured (cause 1).** Capture (step 7 in
`SavestateModule`'s capture, beside `cutbushes`): each tree whose
`LOD_Trees` is disabled with `CurrentView` a `TreeHealth` with
`SpawnedCutTree` true: `CoopTreeId.Id`, `Health`, and each remaining
chunk (`TreeCutChunk` in the view's children, keyed by its parent's
name `1`..`4`) with its `damage`. A new header line, e.g. `chopped =
2543:3:1=2,3=0,4=0;...` - format + parse in `Data/SavestateFile` (pure,
tested: round trip, no line = null, a malformed record skipped). Trees
from `SceneCache.Trees(_treeId)` and NatureKeeper's `TreeLods` cache (no
new scene walk). Restore, both kinds, after the regrow:
- `RegrowTrees` skips a listed tree whose live cut view already matches
  its record (Health, chunk set, damage) - nothing to do, no flicker;
  any other listed tree regrows as today and goes on a pending list.
- Pending trees (Quick load: the ones just regrown; Full load: every
  record) are re-chopped once their standing view is up (`CurrentView`
  a `TreeHealth` with `SpawnedCutTree` false): `DoSpawnCutTree()` on it,
  then on the new `LodTree.CurrentView`: destroy the parent of each chunk
  not in the record, set each listed chunk's `damage` and its Fake1..4
  by the thresholds above (not `Hit`: it spawns particles and its 0.5 s
  `Delay` blocks a second call), set `Health`. Look every 0.5 s from a
  coroutine; the view was up within 2 s of a Full load at 6 m. A tree
  far from the player has no view until he nears it: keep it pending
  for this world, drop the list on the next restore or scene change.
- Log in the restore line: `trees: n half-chopped as captured (m
  waiting for their view)`.
Tests: the record format / parse; the damage -> active Fake mapping as a
pure function (0 -> Fake1, 1.5 -> Fake2, 2 -> Fake3, 3.9 -> Fake4).
