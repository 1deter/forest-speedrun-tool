- **Body slide (live):** the Cave 6 piles are static non-convex
  MeshColliders (`DeadBodyPile_01/_02` greebles, deterministic). Frame
  traces: floor = enter 32.5 -> grounded -> 34 dmg; pile bad spot = enter
  31.6, deflected, grounded on the same contact -> 32 dmg; pile good spot
  = enter 32.1 on a face too steep to ground on, slide (-8, -5, 9.8), new
  enters 6.2 / 3.5, then grounded -> 0 dmg. Grid of 80 drops from y -50:
  floor 20/21 damage; touching a pile 20/45 cancelled; deterministic per
  spot. From y -40 4/4 good spots cancel, from -30 2/4.
- **Multi-thrower (live):** `thrownRockDamage.checkVel` = speed x 1.667
  (fixed step 1/60), so the 12 threshold = 7.2 m/s (15 / 8 m/s knock back,
  6 m/s does not). Hit sphere 2 m, on 0.75 s after launch; shots land
  exactly 2.2 m from the aim point after 2.4-2.6 s (IL). A real
  `throwRocks` shot knocked the player back (25 dmg).
- **Correction (live):** every knockback source also sends
  `lookAtExplosion`, which turns the player to face the source, so the push
  goes straight away from the source - not "out of your back as you faced"
  (the 2026-10-03 bomb tests called `Stats.Explosion` directly). Real timed
  bomb: yaw 0 -> 87.3 and 200 -> 44.1, pushed away from it.
