# T-0424 Quick load: a cannibal or animal already burning at the capture is put out by T-0419 (its fire timer isn't recorded) - keep it burning instead?

Running notes across sessions (docs/harness.md 11a).

- 2026-10-21: 2026-10-21 R-0033 round 2: contract written; forest-researcher (opus/high) researching creature fire state live
- 2026-10-22: researched live (forest-researcher, bridge, v0.24.401, Slot 2 Normal): the gap proved on a surface cannibal and a kept lizard; re-light recipes proved by hand on a regular cannibal (rebuilt after a Quick load), a creepy and a lizard. Build plan below. Game facts: docs/game-notes.md *Fire, and what a Quick load leaves of it* (the T-0424 paragraph).

## Cause (proved live, 2026-10-22, v0.24.401)

A burning creature's state is its `onFire` / `Fire` objects plus two
pending Invokes (`HitFire` repeating, `disableBurn` / `cancelFire` once).
Unity has no way to read an Invoke's time left, and T-0419's FireKeeper puts
every burning creature out after a Quick load, so one burning at the
capture comes back out.

- **Surface cannibal** (`t424b`): woken, `Health` 300, `douseMult` 2,
  `EnemyHealth.Burn`, `disableBurn` re-invoked at 14 s; captured 2 s later
  (`onFire` True), restored 2 s after. Restore line: `fire: 1 put out
  (cannibal x1)`; the rebuilt member (`mutant_male(Clone)00200`, at the
  captured spot, `Health` 291) had `onFire` False, `pmCombat` disabled.
- **Kept lizard** (`t424c`): `Health` 100, `animalHealth.Burn`, captured 4 s
  later (`Burning` True), restored. Restore line: `fire: 1 put out (animal
  x1) | animals: 1 put back (health x1)`; after it `AnimalFireGo` inactive,
  `IsInvoking HitFire` / `cancelFire` False, `Health` 91 for 4 s.
- **The burn's length is a local.** `Burn` picks `Random.Range(7, 14)` (10
  creepy_fat, 5 fireman / boss), `singeBurn` 4, and hands it only to
  `Invoke("disableBurn", num)` (IL: `call MonoBehaviour::Invoke(string,
  float)` at `Burn` IL_0350, `singeBurn` IL_02a0; `Burn` IL_003c is
  `doBeltExplosion`, pass it through). The copy in
  `spawnParticleController.burnDuration` (`num + 7`, by `SendMessage`) is
  **not reliable**: it read 0 / a stale 11 on cannibals whose skeleton was
  inactive (far, or just rebuilt) - SendMessage is not delivered there. A
  singe on a near one read 11 (= 4 + 7) as expected. An animal's is fixed:
  `HitFire` at 3 s then every 3 s, `cancelFire` at 15 s.
- Every lighting path goes through `EnemyHealth.Burn` (molotov, flare,
  fire spread, weapons, flamethrower all `SendMessage("Burn")`); `Burn`
  calls `singeBurn` unless `douseMult > 1`. Both guard the two Invokes with
  `!IsInvoking`, so a burn's end is fixed at its first light.
- Side fact: a **sleeping** cannibal hit by `Burn` dies (`HitReal` takes 100
  while `sleepBOOL`): `Health` 43 -> -56 in 1 s, `kill-enemy`.

**The re-light, proved by hand** (no game method does it without `Hit`,
which staggers and, on a sleeper, kills):
- **Regular cannibal**, the one rebuilt after `t424b` (`onFire` False,
  `pmCombat` disabled): each `Fire[i]` `SetActive(true)`, `onFire = true`,
  `pmCombat.FsmVariables` `onFireBool` = true, `pmCombat.enabled = true`,
  `pmCombat.SendEvent("goToOnFire")`, `InvokeRepeating("HitFire", 0.5, 1)`,
  `Invoke("disableBurn", 11)`. 1 s later `pmCombat` state `burning`, health
  287 -> 275 over 3 s, out at 11 s. On a near one (skeleton active) the
  same plus `spawnParticleController.setFireDuration(13)` spawned
  `EnemyFireBody(Clone)` (`burnDuration` 13), state `burning`, out at 6 s
  as invoked (lit 466.24, out between 471.36 and 472.58). `goToOnFire` is
  a global transition of `action_combatFSM` to `burning`; the game reaches
  it through `Hit` -> `gotHit` -> a `BoolTest onFireBool`.
- **Creepy** (`male_creepy` in Cave 6, kept): `Fire[0]` on +
  `setFireDuration(13)` (`EnemyCreepyFireBody(Clone)`), `onFire = true`,
  `Animator.SetBool("onFireBool", true)`, `pmCombat.SendEvent("goToOnFire")`,
  `StartOnFireEvent()` (private; `onFireEventInstance` set, released by
  `disableBurn`), `HitFire` 0.5 / 1, `disableBurn` 6: health 250 -> 224,
  out at 6 s. Its FSM stayed in `attack` (as the game's own creepy burn
  sends the same event).
- **Lizard** (kept, after `t424c`): `AnimalFireGo` `SetActive(true)`
  (`spawnParticleController.animal` True: `AnimalFire1(Clone)` spawned,
  destroyed again on disable), `InvokeRepeating("HitFire", 0.6, 3)`,
  `Invoke("cancelFire", 9.6)`: `Burning` True, health 91 -> 85 -> 79 -> 73
  every 3 s, out at 9.6 s (lit 732.49, out between 741.58 and 742.60).

## Fix (build plan for forest-dev)

Rule (decisions.md *Mirror essentially everything*; the task's answer
(b)): a creature burning at the capture burns again after the restore
with the time it had left and its next damage tick; T-0419's put-out stays
for everything else.

### 1. Record each light (`Game/FireKeeper`)
- **Cannibals: a transpiler** on `EnemyHealth.Burn` and `singeBurn`
  (DeclaredOnly, as `Patch` does now) replacing each `call
  UnityEngine.MonoBehaviour::Invoke(string, float32)` with `call
  FireKeeper.NoteInvoke(MonoBehaviour, string, float)` (same stack shape).
  `NoteInvoke` calls `self.Invoke(name, t)` first, never throws, and when
  `name == "disableBurn"` stores `EnemyLit[self.GetInstanceID()] =
  (Time.time, t)`. Count the replaced calls; hooked only when each method
  had one `disableBurn` call (`ldstr "disableBurn"` before it). Gotcha 122
  does not apply (not a getter). Keep the existing `EnemyPostfix`.
- **Animals:** a prefix on `animalHealth.Burn`: when `Fire` is inactive and
  `!IsInvoking("cancelFire")`, store `AnimalLit[id] = Time.time` (the end is
  that + 15; a second Burn while burning adds a later `cancelFire`, so the
  first light stays the end).
- Prune with the other registries.

### 2. Capture: a new header line `burning`
Pure `src/Data/BurningStates.cs` (tests): entries joined by `;`,
`e<index>:<left>,<first>` (index into the file's `enemies` list) and
`a<id>:<left>,<first>` (id = AnimalKeeper's root instance id), floats
`0.##` invariant. `FirstTick(elapsed, delay, rate)` = `delay - elapsed`
while `elapsed < delay`, else `rate - (elapsed - delay) % rate` (cannibal
2 / 1, animal 3 / 3). `left = duration - elapsed`; an entry with `left <=
0.05` is not written. Bad entries are dropped on parse. A separate line,
not new fields on `enemies` / `animals`: both decoders reject extra parts,
so an older plugin would drop the whole member / animal.
- `EnemyKeeper.Capture`: for each live cannibal written (index =
  `members.Count` before the Add), its `EnemyHealth` (on the setup's
  GameObject) with `onFire` and an `EnemyLit` entry whose end is ahead.
- `AnimalKeeper.Capture`: each animal with `Fire.activeSelf` and
  `IsInvoking("cancelFire")` and an `AnimalLit` entry.
- `SavestateFile`: `Burning` (written when non-empty, parsed, `Copy()`).
  A creature burning with no record (hooks failed): not written; count it
  in the capture line (`, N burning without a timer`) only when N > 0.

### 3. Restore
- **Cannibals:** `EnemyKeeper.Rebuild` / `RestoreCave` take the file's
  `Burning`; decoding member i attaches its entry to that `EnemyRecord`
  (two in-memory fields, never encoded). In `Place`, after `WriteHealth`,
  `FireKeeper.RelightEnemy(health, left, first)`. This runs after T-0419's
  put-out (that is in the in-place restore; placement is in
  `AfterInPlace`), and also after a Full load's rebuild (the same captured
  state as its health - see the decisions). Add `, K set burning again`
  to the positions note.
- `RelightEnemy`: skip `ai.creepy_boss`. If `onFire`, `disableBurn()` +
  `CancelInvoke("disableBurn")` first. Each `Fire[i]`: `SetActive(true)`
  if inactive, then `SendMessage("setFireDuration", left + 7f,
  DontRequireReceiver)` (boxed float - a far cannibal's skeleton is
  inactive and gets no particles, as with the game's own Burn). `onFire =
  true`; the burnt skin as the light does (`Burnt` onto `MySkin` and
  `setup.propManager.lowSkinnyBody` / `lowBody`'s `SkinnedMeshRenderer`,
  `alreadyBurnt = true`, only when `Burnt` and `MySkin` are set);
  `pmCombat.FsmVariables.GetFsmBool("onFireBool").Value = true`. Creepies
  (`ai.creepy`, `creepy_male`, `creepy_fat`, `creepy_baby`):
  `animator.SetBool("onFireBool", true)`, `pmCombat.SendEvent("goToOnFire")`,
  `StartOnFireEvent()`. Others: `pmCombat.enabled = true`,
  `pmCombat.SendEvent("goToOnFire")`. Then `InvokeRepeating("HitFire",
  first, 1f)` and `Invoke("disableBurn", left)`. FSM access by reflection
  as `DeathSequence` / `EnemyKeeper` do.
- **Animals:** in `AnimalKeeper.HealthStep`, after the health / skin /
  poison writes, an entry for the animal: `FireKeeper.RelightAnimal(ah,
  left, first)` = `Fire.SetActive(true)`; a deer (`spawnFunctions.deer`)
  also `StartOnFireEvent()`; `InvokeRepeating("HitFire", first, 3f)`;
  `Invoke("cancelFire", left)`. Same world and launch only, as the rest
  of AnimalKeeper; it already runs after FireKeeper's put-out. The
  animals note gains `fire xN`.
- `FireKeeper.Restore`'s put-out is unchanged (it puts out what burns now;
  the steps above light the captured ones again).

### Decisions for the author
- A **Full load** lights the captured burning cannibals again too (the
  family rebuild already restores their health and spots; the mirror rule
  says the same for their fire). Animals only on a Quick load, as
  AnimalKeeper already is.
- Gaps left: a second Burn on a burning animal stacks a second `HitFire`
  (double damage) - only the first is put back; a cannibal's burning
  animation / FSM state at the capture is not (it re-enters `burning`
  from the start); a far cannibal gets no flame particles (as in the game).
