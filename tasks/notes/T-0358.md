OPTION for timing: a smooth press (as now) vs exact (frame-true, no animation, no latch) as part of the same switch or its own. Runner-facing words for the switch are the author's (not invented here). Related: T-0356 (the row layout), T-0363 (a dim outline needs a crisp edge).
