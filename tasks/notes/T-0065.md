# T-0065 Savestates: a climbing rope on a wall is ended on a restore, not put back

Running notes across sessions (docs/harness.md 11a).

- 2026-10-18: research (bridge, v0.24.345): the wall climb is unreachable; the 'climbing rope' is the built ClimbingRope (rope action), already captured. Real bugs: every rope restore enters from the top (wrong height / stuck on the ground) and built ropes share one path (wrong rope). Build plan below.

## Cause (proved over the bridge, 2026-10-18, released v0.24.345, Slot 1 Creative)

1. **No wall climb exists to restore.** `PlayerClimbWallAction` is only
   reached from an `activateClimb` / `activateClimbTop` with `climbType ==
   wallClimb`; `find type activateClimb` with inactive objects lists 22, all
   `ropeClimb` (cave ropes, 12 fishing stands, and a built rope). The
   player's **Climbing Rope** (`BuildingTypes.ClimbingRope`, blueprint 49,
   `_builtPrefab` `RopeBuilt`, hangs from a wall / platform anchor) carries
   `activateClimb` / `activateClimbTop` / `exitClimb` all `ropeClimb`,
   `_itemId` 0: it is a normal rope climb (`PlayerClimbRopeAction`,
   `_currentRopeRoot` = `RopeBuilt(Clone)`). The capture already records it
   (log: `back on the rope (RopeBuilt(Clone))`). The investigations entry
   guessed from IL; it was wrong.
2. **Every rope restore enters from the top** (`RopeClimb.Prepare` ->
   `enterClimbRopeTop`). That entry plays the climb-over-the-edge animation
   with root motion (the body slides down several metres from wherever the
   restore put it) and `stickToRope` pins y to `exitTop - 4` for its first
   climbing frames (`_enteredTop`). Measured:
   - built rope (top 92), captured at y 87 -> held at **88.35**;
   - Cave10RopeSection, captured at y 35 -> slid to 28.4, then **51.12**
     (16 m off);
   - built rope, captured at y 82 (6 m above the bottom trigger at 79.47):
     the slide passes `bottom - 3` before `doingClimb` is set (Invoke 2.6 s),
     `stickToRope` breaks and its `exitClimbRopeGround` does nothing -
     **stuck in the climb pose in the ground** (y 74.89, `onRope` true,
     kinematic, no coroutine). This is the "ended on a restore" look.
3. **Built ropes share one path.** `RopeClimb.Capture` stores the root's
   path, `RopeBuilt(Clone)` for every built rope; `GameObject.Find` returns
   the oldest. Captured on a second rope at (445.07, 85, 13.3) -> restored
   onto the first at (432.07, 88.35, -2.7).

**The fix, simulated live with game calls** (the released Prepare leaves a
climb on the captured rope alone): `enterClimbRope(<the rope's
activateClimb transform>)` (the bottom entry), then the restore, then the
captured position set again once the grab is over:
- built rope at 82: restore put 82, the grab's root motion drifted to
  82.78 over ~1.2 s, a set to 82 after that held (1 s later 82.00);
- Cave10RopeSection at 35: 34.99 -> 35.77 (same drift), set to 35 held;
  then `axis Vertical 1 2` climbed to 40.23 and `-1 2` down to 38.52
  (`doingClimb` true, `IsInCaves` true) - a working climb.

## Build plan (forest-dev)

`src/Game/RopeClimb.cs` (only file with game names), call sites in
`src/Modules/SavestateModule.cs` (Quick load ~l.919, Full load ~l.1772).

1. **Pick the rope by place.** `Prepare(string captured, Vector3 at)`: among
   all `activateClimb` components (`Object.FindObjectsOfType`) whose
   `transform.parent` path equals `captured`, take the one whose parent is
   nearest `at` (the file's X/Y/Z, as `RideModes.Nearest`); none -> "not
   found" as now. The "already on the captured rope" shortcut must compare
   the `_currentRopeRoot` instance with that pick, not the path. No header
   change (old files keep working).
2. **Enter from the bottom.** `enterClimbRope(Transform)` (private, on
   `PlayerClimbRopeAction`; IL: `enterClimbRope(Transform)`) on the picked
   `activateClimb`'s transform instead of `enterClimbRopeTop`. Keep
   `Leave()` first (the entry returns early while `climbBool` /
   `doingClimb`).
3. **Put the height back after the grab.** A coroutine started after the
   restore (Quick load: in the restore's completion; Full load: after
   Prepare, which already runs after the pin): wait until the animator's
   layer-0 `tagHash` is the action's `_climbIdleHash` or `_climbHash`
   (private ints, read by reflection) and not in transition, at most ~3 s,
   then `LocalPlayer.Transform.position = at`. Also run it when Prepare left
   the climb alone (same rope). Log its result in the restore line
   ("back on the rope (<name>) at y 35.0").
4. **The rope dismantled since the capture** (a Quick load re-creates it
   after Prepare ran): if the pick finds nothing before the restore, try
   again in the coroutine after it.
5. Update the header comment in RopeClimb (it says the restore keeps the
   player at the captured spot - it did not).

Tests: no pure logic worth a unit test. Worth an e2e journey
(`tests/e2e/07_rope.py`): Slot 1, `tp 489 57.5 -86`, `call <SpecialActions>
PlayerClimbRopeAction.enterClimbRopeTop <Cave10RopeSection/ropeTriggerTop>`,
`wait 5`, `set player Transform.position 488.97,35,-84.92`, `wait 1.5`,
capture; then for Quick and Full load from `tp 428 78.5 -4`: after `wait 4`
the player within 0.3 m of the header position, `AnimControl.onRope` and
`PlayerClimbRopeAction.doingClimb` true.
