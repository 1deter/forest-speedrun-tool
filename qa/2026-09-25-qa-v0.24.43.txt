section = Resetting mid-swing (a practice spot with a start state, axe or stick in hand)
1) Swing, then press F7 (Restart) halfway through. The swing should stop at once: no finishing it after the restart, no weird pose, no view from inside the neck.
seen = ended attack state
2) Same, but press F7 right as you click to swing. It should still stop.
seen = ended attack state
3) After each restart in 1) and 2), click to swing straight away. The swing should come out on the first click.
4) Downward smash (look down, attack), then F7 during it. It should stop and you hold the weapon normally. Seeing your body when looking down is the game's normal pose, not a bug.
seen = ended attack state
5) 1) to 4) again with the spot set to Full load (Practice editor, Start state row).
seen = restoring its start state with a load
