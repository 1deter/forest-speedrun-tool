# QA list - v0.24.43 (2026-09-25)

The first list for the QA team (~3 runners, maks among them). Folds in
maks's unsent v0.24.43 draft and repeats his v0.24.34 list (items 1-5,
15, 19 here), plus the older *Awaiting an in-game check* items from
CLAUDE.md that need hands rather than the author. Keep this file verbatim
to what was sent; answers arrive numbered against it, with the tester's
name.

```
ForestOverlay v0.24.43 - QA list

Before you start:
- Update in game: Updates tab -> Download -> restart. The Updates tab should say "v0.24.43 (installed)" with the changelog under it.
- BepInEx/LogOutput.log is replaced every time the game starts. Copy it (e.g. LogOutput-yourname-1.log) BEFORE you launch again, and send it with your answers.
- Answer by number. "Works" is a useful answer too.
- Split the items between you however you like; two people on one item is fine.
- Something weird that is not on the list? Note roughly when and what you pressed, so it can be found in the log.

Resetting mid-swing (a practice spot with a start state, axe or stick in hand)
1) Swing, then press F7 (Restart) halfway through. The swing should stop at once: no finishing it after the restart, no weird pose, no view from inside the neck.
2) Same, but press F7 right as you click to swing. It should still stop.
3) After each restart in 1) and 2), click to swing straight away. The swing should come out on the first click.
4) Downward smash (look down, attack), then F7 during it. It should stop and you hold the weapon normally. Seeing your body when looking down is the game's normal pose, not a bug.
5) 1) to 4) again with the spot set to Full load (Practice editor, Start state row).

Savestates
6) Practice editor, a spot with a start state: is the Quick load / Full load switch clear? Does Restart do what the switch says?
7) Cave 5: take the first coin pile by the drop, take a savestate, Full load it. The coins should stay gone, also a few seconds later.
8) Megan (if you know the run's audio well): savestate late in her transformation, fight or kill her, Quick load. Do the transformation sounds and music line up with the picture as in a normal run? Nothing should keep playing into the fight.
9) Busy area (your base, cannibals nearby, things built): savestate, play a minute (build, fight, pick things up), Quick load. Tell me anything that is not as it was at the savestate. Already known, no need to report: chopped trees and bushes stay chopped after a Quick load; cannibals after a Full load show up a few seconds late; cave savestates do not put the cannibals back.
10) Savestate, then place a building blueprint (e.g. a wall) so "GATHER LOGS" shows on the HUD, then Quick load. The GATHER line should be gone.
11) After a Quick load, pick up a stick. It should go where it normally goes. Tell me if one ever vanishes (not in hand, not in the inventory).

Timed runs
12) A timed segment with a start state: press F7, then run it. Does the clock start as usual?
13) Run lines should disappear when you Go to a plain spot or a different segment.

Other tabs
14) Inventory tab: open it for the first time after loading a save. It should be filled, not empty.
15) 100% tab -> Write dumps, then send the natureguide_*.txt file(s) it writes.
16) After any load (from the menu, or a reload on death): is there a hitch or freeze about a second after you get control? There should be none.

Deaths
17) Deaths tab, "Reload save on death" and "Also in the boss fight" on: die in the Megan boss fight. It should reload the save instead of waking you up in the boss room.

Endgame (only if you run to the end anyway)
18) Timed run through the ending: say which ending (crash or shutdown), and whether you said goodbye to Timmy or took the raft out of the world. The log is all I need.

Performance
19) If the game ever feels slow (in the background, with run lines or ghosts on, anywhere), note when and send the log.
```

  '<spot>': ...` line should carry `ended attack state '<state>'` when a

  `resetTrigger` path ran (nothing learned yet, e.g. F7 right after a
  load) - ask how soon after loading.
