# Routes

Every current route starts from the **same downloaded save** (speedrun.com
-> *Resources*, the "preferred saves" per difficulty: you spawn in the
same spot by the plane every time), gets the **vault keycard in Cave 6**,
reaches the **vault door** at the bottom of the sinkhole and finishes at
the **red elevator** and the end button. What differs is how you get
there: the *No Explosive Glitch* route clips through walls and skips most
of the caves and the lab; the *Glitchless* route walks the whole way,
fights Megan and lives on stamina.

Everything on this card is the runners' description from their guides
(dates above); the mechanic behind each trick has its own card. The
guides are on speedrun.com -> The Forest -> Guides, with the videos.

## Settings every guide uses

- **Frame rate unlimited** - the panel clip "could not" be done at 60 fps
  (sxczurass); why fps matters for the smash clip is in `smash-clip`.
- Graphics low except textures (sxczurass); brightness / gamma up to see
  in caves (fruich).
- **Jump on the scroll wheel** - parts of the Glitchless run need jumps
  faster than a key press (yirequ: "the only real way you can do that
  without cheating"). Optionally interact on the other scroll direction for
  coins and ropes.
- **Always run diagonally and jump** - W+A / W+D is faster than W alone
  (`movement-tricks`: the input is clamped at 1.1, not 1.0).
- The timer starts on your **first movement** (not the camera) - see
  `categories-and-rules`.

## Any% No Explosive Glitch (Creative) - sxczurass's route, ~5:48 WR

1. **Plane clip.** Press 1 for the axe, back off (Shift+S), crouch, turn to
   the wall, Shift+W, jump, and at the top of the jump smash the axe into
   the ground; open the book on the way through (`smash-clip`).
2. **Zipline.** In the book place the free zipline right after the clip,
   as close to the cave as you can (too far and the use prompt is at the
   wrong end). It is used on the way **back** from the cave (step 6).
3. **Cave 6 in.** Run diagonally, crouch only twice in the low corridor.
4. **Wall for later.** Either place a custom wall now and, coming back,
   use the hole cutter on it to boost to the top (`wall-and-log-boost`), or
   skip the wall and do the next clip.
5. **To the keycard.** A smash clip from a jump, then jump down and **slide
   on the bodies** to take no fall damage (`fall-damage`); stop sprinting,
   interact to pick up the keycard (210), keep moving. **Spam 1** right
   after the pickup - equipping cuts the keycard animation
   (`keycards-and-pickups`).
6. **Out of Cave 6.** A custom wall in a corner, extended, jump on it
   (wall boost); a second panel clip like the first; another custom wall
   boost; crouch twice; out. Take the zipline: hold W+D, look left, spam
   jump (`zipline-boost`).
7. **Cave 6 -> Cave 4** overland, running diagonally and jumping the whole
   way (landmarks: past a small camp, a lake, boats).
8. **Cave 4 (the "K4 skip").** Spam interact to let go of the rope early,
   Shift+A+S, then jump; jump off a ledge holding W+A while falling. **Pass
   the loading trigger** near the wall at the bottom or the rest of the
   caves stay unloaded (`caves-and-loading`). Jump down to the vault door
   and **jump from the lowest point** so the keycard prompt shows at once;
   use keycard 210 (`endgame-gate`).
9. **Endgame.** A smash clip, a jump avoiding the fall damage animation,
   then either
   - the **normal way**: through the corridors (slide through one, crouch
     jump, uncrouch) to the elevator corridor, or
   - the **lab skip**: up a rock, along a "line", seven jumps on invisible
     collision, invisible ledges, another smash clip into the elevator
     corridor (`lab-skip`).
10. **Red elevator.** Three jumps, hug the left wall, look right, press the
    button, get in, press the next one. The **elevator skip** is done
    blind: hold W+D, turn 45 degrees, crouch, Shift+W, axe smash looking
    down, jump, uncrouch while spamming jump - the "elevator boost"
    (`elevator-skip`). Run to the end button.

## Any% Glitchless (Peaceful / Normal / Hardmode) - yirequ's route, ~18:54 WR

Creative "uses a completely different route" (yirequ). The run lives on
**stamina**: sodas, energy (stamina) mixes, and never sprinting when it
does not pay.

**The shift reset** - the one mechanic the run "is not going to work
without": **release sprint before every animation the game plays** (cave
entrances and exits, ropes, the vault door). Held through it, stamina
does not regenerate during the animation; released, it does [runner].

1. **Start.** Eat the plane meal (the start; `endgame-splits`), look
   down, pick up the soda **second** (otherwise the hotkeys end up wrong),
   then the other pickups. A stick before any rocks.
2. **Cave 5 (rebreather).** Two ways in: let go of the rope and fall
   straight (safe), or Shift+A+W off the front-left edge (faster, picks up
   the coins on the way out instead). A crouch-jump into a "triangle",
   fall damage avoided by hugging edges, a timed left turn before landing.
   Pick up the **rebreather** only (no tank). Swing to break the barrier,
   grab the rope in the air. The **wall climb**: jumps up the rock with
   sprint only for the first part, then the scroll-wheel jump spammed until
   you are launched up (`wall-and-log-boost` for why rock pushes you up).
   Coins on the way; aim for ~620+ coins by the soda machine.
3. **Cave 5 -> Cave 6.** Four flowers on the way (for the stamina mixes),
   rocks for the endgame, files.
4. **Cave 6 (keycard).** Fall damage avoided by landing on a rock, then on
   the "hole in the bodies" while spamming jump (`fall-damage`). On Peaceful
   break the door yourself; on Normal / Hard wait for the cannibals to break
   it while blocking. Keycard 210 on a body. Two **wall climbs** (no
   sprint, scroll jump, crouch only at the top of the second). Then craft:
   **5 stamina mixes** (the fifth on a hotkey) and a **spear** (two sticks).
5. **Cave 6 -> the sinkhole.** Three sodas on the way. The **sinkhole
   jump**: land with the left foot just outside the water line; more than
   half a stamina bar before the jump.
6. **Swim and the Hell Cave.** Swim down without sprint, sprint only when
   swimming level, leave the water on a full bar (`swimming`). A jump where
   the rock "bumps you up a little" - jump right after the bump.
7. **"Sahara" and the vault door.** Turn right for a box of **4 sodas**
   (open it with the spear or a stick). Cannibals here on Normal / Hard -
   be quick, block if they catch you. Along a ridge to the **vault door**
   (keycard 210, `endgame-gate`). Inside: ropes grabbed in the air, a jump
   landing on a box to avoid fall damage, then the first **soda machine**:
   buy 7 at 100 coins each (+100 coins on the counter behind). A pipe jump,
   a crouch section, a drop (fall straight, or jump towards the middle).
   Glitchless forbids *clipping into* Sahara; this route walks in.
   (Where the guide's "Sahara" chapter starts and the door's place in it
   are read from the commentary; the video was not watched.)
8. **Timmy** - grab him from behind the barrier, no need to walk round
   (`endgame-splits`).
9. **Megan.** Hold A then W at the screen shake to stand slightly left of
   her, and throw the spear from her **blind spot**, correcting with A and W
   after every throw or two (each throw pushes you back); ~10 hits. The
   blind spot is bigger towards the back - too close and she spins
   (`megan-boss`). yirequ: about 7 times in 10 with practice.
10. **Back out.** Carry her through the **second** room (cuts a corner),
    put her in, look down for the gold keycard (242), through the gold door,
    a second swim, the endgame soda machine (only if a soda is short), a
    crouch-jump through a slot, the **red elevator** (if its button will not
    show, look away and back), the end button twice.

**Soda maths** (yirequ): five sodas at the start. One on the Cave 5 -> 6
crossing, three to the sinkhole, one after the swim, then stamina mixes
to the box of four, two of those at once, three sodas and a mix from the
machine to Timmy, and the rest back to the elevator. With
620-700 coins you buy all 7 at the first machine; ~500 means 6 (+1 at the
endgame machine); ~400 means 5 and a detour for two more (3-4 s lost).
"Less than 500, probably not even worth playing the run." The run ends on
zero sodas in every case.

**Practising**: yirequ keeps practice saves for the hard parts (Cave 5,
the Cave 6 climbs, the Hell Cave jump, the sinkhole jump) - and suggests
learning the overland paths in a practice game with the developer console's
speed (`speedyrun`) and a tent placed at the destination as a beacon
(practice only; the console is banned in runs - `dev-console-and-creative`).

## The 2023 Creative route (fruich) - for history

The same skeleton as sxczurass's route (plane clip, zipline, Cave 6 with
custom walls and edge bugs, Cave 4's K4 skip, a panel clip at the vault
door, the elevator skip), timed by hand in LiveSplit and retimed by the
moderators. Differences worth knowing:
- **Edge bugs**: landing on an edge cancels the fall damage animation
  (~1-1.5 s). In Creative you take no damage but still lose the animation
  time; on Normal the K4 skip **needs** the fall damage avoided to survive
  (`fall-damage`).
- The elevator skip in three versions (one with a rare extra boost); a
  book opened at the last moment rescues a failing clip (`elevator-skip`).
- A **blind lab route** 2-5 s faster existed, done with the lab unloaded
  ("nobody wants to blindfold") - see `lab-skip` for why the collision is
  there without the visuals.
- fruich used Mod API for the recording (no landing stumble); it is a cheat
  in runs.

## Unrestricted Any% (Creative, bombs) - not covered

The 3:31 world record uses **bomb boosts** (`bomb-boost`); no guide on
speedrun.com describes its route, and it has not been retraced here.
